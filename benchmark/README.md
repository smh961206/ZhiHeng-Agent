# ZhiHeng Benchmark

Benchmark is an executable definition of acceptable research quality.

Initial categories:
- Quick Screen
- Earnings Update
- Deep Research
- Company Comparison
- Vision/Table
- Complex Valuation
- Missing/Conflict

Later categories:
- Retrieval
- Fact normalization
- Knowledge applicability
- Claim/counter-evidence
- Forecast
- Belief calibration
- Decision
- Portfolio
- Replay
- Red Team

Quality gates dominate cost scores.
