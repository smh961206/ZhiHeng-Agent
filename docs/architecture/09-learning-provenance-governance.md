# Learning / Provenance / Governance

## Reproducibility

A formal research snapshot eventually freezes:
- app version;
- model policy;
- Knowledge snapshot;
- ontology;
- evidence/fact snapshot;
- market cutoff;
- formula versions.

## Learning

Decision → Outcome → Attribution → Calibration → Failure Analysis → Knowledge/Policy Proposal.

Production is not allowed to mutate Knowledge or Policy online without offline benchmark + human approval.

## Audit

Human overrides, fact revisions, Knowledge publication, decision creation and critical model escalations become auditable events.

## Institutional memory

Store structured:
- research memory;
- failure memory;
- conflict memory;
- decision memory.

Do not turn this into free-form chat memory.

## H0 calibration

CURRENT: job/checkpoint payloads, public receipts, saved rule provenance and Knowledge snapshots. FUTURE: complete frozen research manifests, calibrated learning, canonical audit ledger and human-override records. No online self-modifying learning platform exists.
